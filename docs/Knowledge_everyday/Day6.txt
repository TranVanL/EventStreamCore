Day 6 : QNX Foundation 

1.QNX is microkernel OS . It only retain the indispensable component such as scheduling , thread/process management , interrupt handling , synchronization , timer and message passing. 
Other massive policy/service like filesystem , network , device driver run under user-space process or system service .

-> Purpose for this design : 
- Diminish trusted computing base (Crucial component that must run correctly , anything wrong will crash all system )
+ Linux has a huge TCB because Linux is monolithic kernel : Kernel space has scheduler , memory management , filesystem , network , driver ,....
Anyone of them stuck in trouble ,even a minor bug -> Boom , everything explode 
+ QNX has microkernel push almost to user space , only  keep component such as scheduling , thread/process management , interrupt handling , synchronization , timer and message passing.  
-> The weight significantly decrease 

- Giảm blast radius khi một driver hoặc service lỗi.
- Isolation service so avoid crash domino , support restart service 
- Increase precditable latency ability (with OS designed for decrease jitter and unpredictable latency from kernel handle ) 

Drawback : 
-**Most important is IPC . Heart of QNX but it also is flaw of QNX . 
IPC overhead , everything work through message passsing with flow from user->kernel->user . (system call , copy , ....) 
Context switching : IPC system call , wake up receiver , reply wake up sender 
- Driver complicated , ecosystem limited , RAM usage ,....
